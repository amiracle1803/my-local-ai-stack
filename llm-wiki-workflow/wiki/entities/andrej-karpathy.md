# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator who popularized the [[concepts/llm-wiki-workflow]] pattern through a viral tweet (April 3, 2026) and a follow-up GitHub gist "idea file" (April 4, 2026).

## Background

Co-founder of OpenAI and former Director of AI at Tesla. Coined the term "vibe coding" (using natural language to drive code generation). Runs a widely followed blog at karpathy.bearblog.dev. Author of neural network educational resources including the "Zero to Hero" series.

His 2025 blog post "The Append and Review Note" described an append-only notes file reviewed and reorganized periodically — an earlier, simpler version of the LLM wiki idea.

## Relevance

Karpathy's April 2026 tweet "LLM Knowledge Bases" described how he uses LLMs to build personal knowledge wikis instead of just generating code. It garnered ~8 million views and became one of the most-discussed AI workflow posts of the year.

The follow-up gist introduced the [[concepts/idea-file]] format: share the pattern, not the implementation. He deliberately left it "intentionally a little bit abstract/vague" so that each user's LLM agent could adapt it.

His personal setup: LLM agent open on one side, Obsidian on the other. The LLM makes edits while he browses the results in real time — following links, checking the graph view, reading updated pages.

His research wiki at the time of the gist: ~100 articles, ~400,000 words on a single ML research topic.

Key quote: "Obsidian is the IDE; the LLM is the programmer; the wiki is the codebase."

Key observation on maintenance: "The tedious part of maintaining a knowledge base is not the reading or the thinking — it's the bookkeeping. Humans abandon wikis because the maintenance burden grows faster than the value. LLMs don't get bored, don't forget to update a cross-reference, and can touch 15 files in one pass."
