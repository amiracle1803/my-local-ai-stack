# Welcome to the sample vault

This folder stands in for your real Obsidian vault so everything runs out of
the box. When you're ready, open `config.json` and set `vault_path` to your
actual vault instead.

Anything the AI generates lands in the `_generated/` subfolder. Your own notes
are never modified.

Try adding notes with tasks, decisions, and observations (see `Daily/` for an
example), then run `project2-second-brain\start.bat` to watch it build your
task list, decision log, and a daily review.
