Dawn Raid

Kess crouched on the rooftop of the old clocktower, a young scout with wind-tangled brown hair and a worn green scarf. Below, the harbor was already busy with iron ships.

"The signal flare goes up in one minute," whispered Brann beside her — a large man with a shaved head and a dented brass pauldron. "When it fires, you run. Don't look back."

"If the flare fails, I climb down and light it myself," Kess said, checking the lantern at her belt. "Same as always."

Brann shook his head, smiling despite himself. "You are the worst scout I've ever trained. And the best."

The flare screamed upward, painting the harbor red. Kess ran.
